prometheus-pve-exporter {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is deployed as service {{ include "prometheus-pve-exporter.fullname" . }} on port {{ .Values.service.port }}.

1. Reach the exporter from inside the cluster at:
  http://{{ include "prometheus-pve-exporter.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}

2. Or port-forward it to your machine:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "prometheus-pve-exporter.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

3. Endpoints:
  Proxmox VE metrics (scrape target):
    curl 'http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/pve?target=<proxmox-host>&module=default&cluster=1&node=1'
  Exporter self-metrics:
    curl 'http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/metrics'
